# Third-party notes

Unity Editor, engine modules, Unity UI and the Unity Test Framework are obtained through Unity/Package Manager and retain their upstream licenses. They are not included as source or binaries in this repository. The resolved package list is recorded in `Packages/packages-lock.json`.

The original local project also contained Super Retro Collection / its extension, FangAutoTile and an RPGPowerForge music pack. Their graphics, prefabs, scripts, music, sample scenes and other distributable contents are excluded. The public starter does not require these packages.

The original code's optional integration checks for `CharacterAppearance` and `PlayerMovement` remain as string-based component lookups. They do not bundle those implementations. Users who add their own asset packs are responsible for the applicable pack terms.

`Assets/Workshop/Art/WorkshopSquare.png` is a generated plain white square from the included `WorkshopShapes` source. Other simple effects and sounds are generated by the runtime code. No commercial media is substituted into the public scenes.
